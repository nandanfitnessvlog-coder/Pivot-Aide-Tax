## ⚡ 3. The 4 Resolution Tiers (Audit & Notice Desk)

Replace the single bullet point on the live site (*"Small Business IRS Audit Support"*) with the 4 distinct resolution tiers:

1. **Tier 1 — Notice Response (`$450` per notice)**:
   * Covers CP2000, CP14, CP501–504, LT11, and state equivalents.
   * *Copy*: *"We read the letter, evaluate whether the IRS or state agency is correct, draft and submit the response, and track the case to closure."*
2. **Tier 2 — Examination Representation (`From $2,500`)**:
   * Form 2848 Power of Attorney. Correspondence, office, or field audits. Client does not attend; we interface directly with the examiner.
3. **Tier 3 — Collections & Relief (`From $1,500`)**:
   * Transcript analysis, Installment Agreements, Currently Not Collectible (CNC) status, Penalty Abatement, Offers in Compromise (OIC).
4. **Tier 4 — Back Years & Non-Filers (`$350/yr + $500 reconstruction`)**:
   * Wage & income transcripts, SFR reversal, multi-year back returns.
5. **Add-on: Audit Shield (`$75/year`)**:
   * Included Tier 1 & Tier 2 exam protection for all returns prepared by Pivot Aide Tax.
