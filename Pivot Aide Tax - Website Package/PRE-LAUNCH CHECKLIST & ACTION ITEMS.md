```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

│  Bookkeeping, payroll, sales tax, quarterly estimates, retainers       │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Layer 1: The Storefront ($100 – $1,250/return)                        │
│  Transactional individual & business filing, extensions, amendments    │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Layer 0: Complimentary Services ($0)                                  │
│  Second Look, Notice Triage, Consultations, Entity Fit Check           │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
  ▲ Cross-Cutting: Audit & Resolution ($450 – quoted per matter)
```

> **The Conversion Principle**: Any client on a monthly bookkeeping plan receives their entity return at half price ($375 / $625 savings). This transforms a one-off $750 seasonal filer into an annual $4,500+ client.

## 🦉 Uncle Ed Persona & Brand Voice Rules (Exhibits I & J)

* **Who Uncle Ed Is**: A Great Horned Owl dressed in a charcoal jacket with brand blue eyes (`#019FFF`). Experienced, calm, dry, warm, and highly competent.
* **The 5 Voice Rules**:
  1. **Calm First**: Never panic, never leverage fear.
  2. **Plain Words**: *"The IRS thinks you left off $8,400 of income"* (never *"CP2000 underreporter inquiry"*).
  3. **Short Sentences**: Conversational tone, not legal briefs.
  4. **Honest About Bad News**: Tell the truth plainly if tax is owed; credibility is everything.
  5. **Dry, Never Jokey**: Warm and reassuring, never cartoonish comic relief.
* **Non-Negotiable Guardrail**: Defend against the notice, never attack the government. Zero anti-government rhetoric, zero "pennies on the dollar" gimmicks.
