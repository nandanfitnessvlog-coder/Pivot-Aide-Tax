Prepared 1 September 2026


WHAT IS IN THIS FOLDER
----------------------

1 - Site Prototype (open in any browser).html
    The working prototype of the restructured pivotaidetax.com.
    Double-click it. It opens in Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox on
    Windows, Mac or Linux. Nothing to install.

    Nine sections across the top: Home, File Taxes, Tax Strategy,
    Audit & Resolution, Business, Free Help, Resources, Appointments,
    Meet Uncle Ed. Click between them.

    Everything is inside this one file - no images folder, no
    stylesheet, nothing to lose when you email or copy it.

2 - Site Prototype (PDF version).pdf
    The same nine sections as a PDF, one long page each, for anyone
    who would rather not open an HTML file, or who wants to mark it up.
    Opens in Preview, Acrobat, or a browser.

3 - Service Architecture and Uncle Ed Brand File.pdf
    The 13-page written strategy behind the prototype: what is wrong
    with the current site, the four-layer offering model, pricing,
    the audit and resolution tiers, the Uncle Ed persona and voice
    rules, the revised navigation, and the decisions still open.


THE STANDING FILE
-----------------

The flagship engagement is on the Tax Strategy section. It is the
year-round package for high-income individuals and business owners:
a dedicated advisor, four scheduled strategy sessions a year, a
written strategy plan, a mid-year projection, unlimited questions,
and every return, estimate and notice handled inside one annual fee.

Two tracks - Track A for high-income individuals, Track B for
business owners and entities.

No price is published. It is quoted after a free 45-minute scoping
call and a Second Look at three prior returns. It is deliberately the
only offer on the site without a number next to it.


HOW TO SEND THIS TO SOMEONE ELSE
--------------------------------

Send the whole folder, or the .zip file that sits beside it. If you
are emailing only one file, send the PDF (item 2) - some email systems
strip .html attachments.


A NOTE ON FONTS
---------------

The prototype uses three web fonts (Fraunces, Poppins, JetBrains Mono)
loaded from Google Fonts. On a computer with internet they load
automatically. With no internet, the page falls back to system fonts -
the layout, colours and content are unaffected, only the lettering
changes slightly. Nothing breaks either way.


BEFORE ANY OF THIS GOES LIVE
----------------------------

Three things are still open, and two of them are decisions only you
can make. They are set out in full on the last page of the strategy
file (Exhibit L):

  1. Which Circular 230 credential covers IRS representation, and
     whose name signs the Form 2848. This gates the whole Audit &
     Resolution section - and The Standing File, which includes
     examination representation. Do not publish either until this
     is answered.

  2. Business return pricing. The prototype shows $750 and $1,250;
     the live site currently shows $550 for both. Proposed, not
     confirmed.

  3. The legal standing of Pivot Aide Tax - its own registered entity,
     or an assumed name of Pivot Aide Consulting. The brands now
     present separately, but if it is an assumed name the legal name
     still has to appear somewhere a client can find it, normally the
     terms and the engagement letter.

The Uncle Ed owl in the prototype is a placeholder mark. The art
direction for the real thing is in Exhibit J of the strategy file.

Tax figures on the Tax Resources section are for tax year 2026 and
come from IRS Revenue Procedure 2025-32, IRS Notice IR-2025-111,
Revenue Procedure 2025-19 and the 2026 standard mileage announcement.
They will need refreshing when the IRS publishes the 2027 numbers.
